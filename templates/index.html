Great Personalities of India

THE ARCHITECT OF THE INDIAN CONSTITUTION

Dr. B. R. Ambedkar

14 April 1891 – 6 December 1956

Dr. Bhimrao Ramji Ambedkar was an Indian jurist, economist and social reformer. He served as the Chairman of the Drafting Committee of the Constituent Assembly and was the first Law Minister of independent India.

His contributions focused on constitutional values, equality, social justice and the rights of citizens.
